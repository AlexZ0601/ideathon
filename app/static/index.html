How it works

01

Describe the blocker

Plain language, the way you'd explain it to a friend. "Our cathode loses capacity after 300 cycles." No taxonomy to learn, no dropdowns.

02

Get evidence, not scores

Each card names the one paper that drove the match and says in a sentence how it connects. A bare "92% match" tells you nothing you can act on.

03

Send a real email

Shortlist who looks right, then generate an intro that cites their actual work. Specific beats generic — that's the difference between a reply and silence.

Why this isn't another matching app

Every marketplace dies of the cold start problem: empty on both sides, nobody comes back. ResearchBridge doesn't have one, because one side is already populated.

Princeton research output is public and indexed — papers, abstracts, topics, citation records. We built the entire supply side from OpenAlex before a single user existed. Open the app on day one and there are 4,159 researchers in it.

It also means faculty never have to do anything. They don't install an app, build a profile, or check a queue — which matters, because they wouldn't. Founders are the only active side. Researchers are discoverable from work they already published years ago.

What this doesn't do

  • Public data only. Nobody here opted in, verified their profile, or agreed to be contacted. Treat a match as a lead, not an endorsement.
  • It's a discovery aid, not a recommendation. Semantic similarity finds plausible people; judging fit is still your job.
  • Departments are approximated. OpenAlex has no department field, so we infer a field from each researcher's dominant topic. Expect the occasional odd label.
  • Seniority is a heuristic. "Established PI" comes from h-index and career span, not a directory. About a third of authors have no author record and are ranked neutrally.
  • Authors include grad students and postdocs, not just faculty. That's often useful — they reply more — but it isn't always who you wanted.
  • Drafted emails are drafts. Read before sending, and look up the real address yourself. We never guess contact details.
