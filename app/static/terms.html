Terms & Privacy

cofoundr is a student project built for the PrincetonBuilds Ideathon. It is not a registered company, it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Princeton University, and it carries no warranty of any kind. Read this before you use it, and especially before you contact anyone through it.

1. What this service does

cofoundr searches publicly available research metadata to suggest people whose published work relates to a problem you describe, generates draft outreach messages, and can pass messages between accounts. It is a discovery and drafting aid. It does not verify people, vouch for anyone, or guarantee a reply.

2. Where the researcher data comes from

Researcher names, affiliations, publication titles, abstracts, and citation statistics come from OpenAlex, an open catalogue of scholarly works, and are used under its open licence. Company and post data in the White Space Map come from public Y Combinator listings and the public Hacker News API.

Researchers listed here did not sign up and have not consented to being listed. They appear because their work is published and publicly indexed. We show only professional, already-public information: name, field, papers, and citation counts. We do not collect, infer, or display home addresses, personal contact details, demographics, or anything about a person's private life.

3. Removal and correction

If you are listed and want to be removed, or something about you is wrong, say so and we will remove or correct it. There is no appeal process to exhaust and no account required. Because the index is rebuilt from OpenAlex, a correction at the source will also flow through on the next rebuild. Removal requests are honoured for the app regardless.

4. Your account and your data

  • We store your name, email, a hashed password, the profile fields you fill in, any resume you upload, and the messages you send.
  • Your resume is parsed to text and used only to help draft your own outreach. It is not shown to other users and not shared with anyone.
  • Passwords are hashed with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 and never stored in plain text.
  • We do not sell data, run advertising, or share anything with third parties beyond the AI provider described below.
  • Ask and we will delete your account and everything attached to it.

5. AI-generated content

Draft emails, match explanations, jargon translations, and White Space Map cluster labels are generated by a large language model (OpenAI). To produce them, the relevant text — your problem statement, profile, resume excerpt, or message — is sent to OpenAI's API.

Generated text can be wrong. It may misstate what a paper says or what you meant. You are responsible for reading anything before you send it. A draft is a draft.

6. Rules for contacting people

By using cofoundr to contact anyone, you agree to:

  • Send genuine, individual messages. No bulk or automated outreach.
  • Be honest about who you are and what you want.
  • Accept silence as an answer. Do not repeatedly contact someone who has not replied.
  • Respect an opt-out. Researchers can mark themselves as not open to intros.

Harassment, spam, misrepresentation, or scraping this service will get an account removed without notice.

7. Matching is not advice

Rankings come from embedding similarity between your text and published abstracts. Semantic proximity is a search signal, not an assessment of anyone's competence, availability, or fit. "Gaps" in the White Space Map indicate an absence of similar text in the data we collected — which may mean nobody built it, or may simply mean nobody wants it. None of this is business, investment, legal, career, or academic advice.
